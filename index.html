§ 1What I do

Security and compliance work that closes the loop

I work with security, engineering and leadership teams to turn audit findings and control gaps into resolved issues, not open items on a spreadsheet.

Risk assessments and gap analysis

Quantitative and qualitative assessments that find control deficiencies and rank them for remediation.

Compliance monitoring

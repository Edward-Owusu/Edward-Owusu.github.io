§ 4About me

I started my career in governance, risk and compliance in Ghana, maintaining risk registers and auditing systems for a cold storage enterprise. I came to the United States for my master's degree at Saginaw Valley State University, and I now work as a GRC Analyst in the United States.

I believe good security shouldn't depend on the size of a company's budget. That's why I build my compliance tools in the open, so clinics, small manufacturers and local organizations can use the same frameworks larger companies rely on.
